The Automated Lifecycle

1

Observe

Capture or upload a photo of a waste, water, energy, or grounds issue.

2

AI Classify

Gemini analyzes the image for category, description, and severity in real-time.

3

Score & Route

Deterministic rules compute a 0-100 priority score and assign a department.

4

Remediate

Staff accepts task, starts work, and uploads an "after" resolution photo.

5

AI Verify

Gemini compares before and after scenes to automatically resolve or review the task.
